AI caption generator

Ready

Turns audio or video into timestamped SRT and WebVTT files without uploading anything. Built on Hugging Face Transformers.js — the open-source library that runs state-of-the-art machine learning directly in your browser — and OpenAI Whisper, the most widely used open-source speech recognition model. Drop in an MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, WebM or MOV file and the tool decodes the audio track, runs Whisper locally with segment-level timestamps, and produces ready-to-use subtitles you can preview, copy, or download.

Because the model runs on your own device via WebAssembly, your media is never uploaded, logged, or stored — safe for unpublished videos and confidential recordings. The model downloads once on first use and is cached by your browser.
